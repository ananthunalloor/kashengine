Instruments

The symbols that we download (Yahoo Finance symbols). A global cue counts in the prediction. Its weight is how much it counts. A plus sign means "a rise is good for Indian stocks". A minus sign means "a rise is bad" (oil, the dollar, fear). Its scale is the move in percent that is a full signal. The numbers are a first guess. Change them only when the accuracy numbers (Metrics) give a reason.{% if not request.user.is_superuser %} Only a superuser can change them.{% endif %}

The weights of the active cues add up to {{ weight_total }} (without signs). The prediction divides by this sum, so it does not need to be 1.
