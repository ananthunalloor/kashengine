How to read a schedule

Five fields: minute hour day-of-month month day-of-week. A star means "every". */3 means every third. 7-19/2 means every second hour from 7 to 19. mon-fri means Monday to Friday. Example: 30 7 * * mon-fri is 07:30 on weekdays.
